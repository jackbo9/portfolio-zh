Dossier thesis

Evidence matters when
it changes the structure.

Promap is about career-stage differences changing product direction. UniPlus is about turning multiple roles, modules, and service relationships into an understandable platform structure.

Together they show one practice: observe difference, model relationships, make a structural decision, and use prototypes to test whether it can be used.

Supporting case 03 / Promap

One platform is not
one career journey.

Twenty-plus interviews and five personas showed that workers at different career stages faced different goals, barriers, and support networks.

EvidenceDifferent career realities

Goals and barriers varied by stage.

InterpretationA universal feature list hides difference

Features alone could not explain value by stage.

DecisionOrganize strategy by career stage

Personas and journeys shaped MVP and roadmap discussion.

BoundaryDirection, not deployment proof

No adoption or business-growth claim.

Supporting case 04 / UniPlus

A service ecosystem becomes
a product through structure.

The academic project contained multiple roles, credentials, lifecycle states, and workflows. The key decision was to model boundaries and information flow before designing screens.

EvidenceRoles across a lifecycle

Actors needed different information and actions at different stages.

InterpretationMenus cannot replace a system model

Screen-first design would hide permissions and dependencies.

DecisionDefine modules, permissions, workflows first

Interfaces were derived from the service structure.

BoundaryAcademic prototype

No deployment or organizational adoption claim.

Retrospective

Keep the decisions.
Edit the process theatre.

What remains relevant

Recognizing differences, modeling relationships, and turning them into product structure still defines my work.

What I would change

I would prototype critical assumptions earlier and validate whether the structure actually helps people complete tasks.
